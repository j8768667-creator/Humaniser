03 / Tone & Humanise Controls

Moves are deliberately moderate: small EQ moves sound natural, big ones sound processed
55%
EQ tilt (±5.5 dB). Below 50% = closer & more forward · above 50% = rounder & warmer. Not a true formant shifter.
45%
Dynamic de-esser above 5.5 kHz (only acts on harsh peaks) + gentle 3 kHz / 7 kHz dips
40%
Up to +8 dB at 150 Hz and a 4 dB cut at 300 Hz to avoid mud
0%
Two softly detuned copies panned hard L/R behind the lead
20%
Random slow pitch drift plus a vibrato whose depth and speed wander. Keep low on already-expressive vocals
15%
Vowel colour slowly wanders at random (three moving mid-range resonances) so sustained notes don't sound frozen
50%
40% = neutral · 0% = −4 dB darker · 100% = +5 dB gentle air (kept low on purpose: it also lifts AI fizz)
40%
Parallel compression evens out quiet words; light tape warmth. Clean at 0%
15%
Soft, voice-shaped breath (mid-range, no hiss) that follows the singing, so notes feel breathed rather than generated
30%
Random pitch jitter, volume shimmer and wandering vowel colour: the tiny irregularities real voices have and AI voices lack
12%
0% = dry booth · 25% = natural room · 60%+ = ballad hall
