04 / What Changed in V4

Zero Comb-Filtering & Dynamic Sibilance Control: Earlier versions blended an un-delayed dry voice with a delayed pitch-modulated signal, which caused hollow phase cancellation (comb-filtering). V4 keeps the core vocal 100% phase-coherent, adds a Dynamic High-Frequency Cushion that only ducks harsh notes when they spike, and includes built-in 320 kbps / 192 kbps MP3 encoding alongside 24-bit WAV.
